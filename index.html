Xin Wang

AI Researcher

Oxford, United Kingdom

My interests mainly lie in large language model (LLM) post-training, adaptation, reasoning, and multimodal learning, with applications in healthcare, biomedicine, and social computing. My long-term goal is to develop AI systems that are not only more capable, but also more understandable and better aligned with human needs and values.

For the most recent publications, please visit my Google Scholar page.

I am currently a Researcher at The Alan Turing Institute and a Visiting Researcher at the University of Oxford, where I previously worked as a postdoctoral researcher with Prof. David Clifton. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University under the supervision of Prof. Ling Feng.

Outside of research, I enjoy climbing and travelling — I hope to explore as much of the world as I can!
